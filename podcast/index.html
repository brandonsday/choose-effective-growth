Agenda

30 MINUTES
How the 30 minutes go.
5–7 MIN · OFF RECORDING
Introduction + Audio Check
  • We say hi, I check your audio
  • Questions to check your audio:
    • Where did you grow up?
    • How many siblings, and where do you fall in the birth order?
    • What are you really into right now outside of work?
  • Confirmation on 2 things:
    • Do you have a fun fact you can share to intro the podcast?
    • Sharing numbers
10–15 MIN · RECORDING
We Hit Record
  • I want it to feel like people are eavesdropping into a conversation we are having
  • My primary focus is to let you add as much value through your amazing experience as possible
  • The questions below are just a starting point and we can riff on whatever we want out of them
5–8 MIN · OFF RECORDING
Conclude + send off
  • We did it!
  • Loose ends, and I ask who else I should talk to
  • I'll send you the episode when it goes live
  • You get the raw audio and video too, use it however you want, clip it, post it, no permission needed
